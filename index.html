    Meet the AI Career Coach

    The fastest way into everything on the portal. Ask a question in plain language and it finds the right page, guide or event, any time of day.

    Where to find it

    On the home page, look for the wide red button in the right-hand column. On every other page, a small round red button sits in the lower-right corner.

    Good to know

    • It searches only what Career Services has published, not the open internet.
    • If there's no match, it says so and points you to a coach instead of guessing.
    • Use it to prepare for an appointment, not to replace one.
