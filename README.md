# Elevate Portal Alumni Tour

An interactive, presentation-ready tour of [elevate.iit.edu](https://elevate.iit.edu/) for Illinois Tech alumni, prepared for IIT-4A (African American Alumni Association of Illinois Tech).

**Live:** https://elevateillinoistech.github.io/elevate-portal-alumni-tour/

## Presenting
- Use the ← → arrow keys (or Back / Next) to move between the 10 stops. Press `/` to search every link.
- "Full screen" for the projector. "✦ AI Career Coach" jumps to the spotlight stop.
- Deep links: add `#stop3` (etc.) to the URL.

## Data sources
- U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Employee Tenure (Sept 24, 2026)
- BLS Occupational Outlook Handbook, Fastest Growing Occupations (2025–35)
- Pew Research Center, "6 facts about America's STEM workforce" (Apr 14, 2021)
- WFH Research, Survey of Working Arrangements and Attitudes (Sept 2026 update)
- Illinois Tech Career Services, First Destination Survey response rates (Class of 2025)
